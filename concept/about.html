Hello again

I’m Melissa. I live by the sea in Bangor with my husband, our toddler and Orca—an Australian Shepherd who has never knowingly been left out of a photograph.

Say hello →

Outside product design, photography is how I slow down and pay attention. I’m drawn to animals, birds, family life and the small moments that would otherwise vanish. I like the idea that one good frame can be enough.

I’m also a collector by nature: Pokémon cards, screenshots from Nintendo games, facts about dog breeds, books I mean to recommend to everyone, and ideas for tiny websites that may or may not become real.

None of this is separate from how I work. Curiosity, pattern recognition and noticing what other people miss are useful everywhere.
